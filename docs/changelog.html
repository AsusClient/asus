1.1.0 — Themes & capes

Published

1.1.0

Current13 Nov 2026
  • Ten new themes. Midnight, Inferno, Cyberpunk, Emerald, Bloodmoon, Lavender, Arctic, Sunset, Obsidian and Amethyst Night join the original six. Each has its own palette, borders, text colours, gradients and animated backdrop.
  • Fifteen original capes. Voidfall, Crimson Emperor, Oceanic, Frostbite, Celestial, Bloodmoon, Sakura, Infernal, Phantom, Emerald Knight, Aurora, Royal, Midnight, Cyber and Dragonless, drawn on the vanilla cape atlas.
  • Cape maker library. Save, load and remove capes; import and export a compact cape code; keep undo and redo across edits.
  • Dragon cosmetics paused. Dragon models, rendering and menu entries are disabled. The code is kept for a later restoration; other cosmetics are unaffected.
  • Dia SMP work. Pathfinding, stuck recovery, hole and web escape, water decision checks, Auto Bubble Web, rotation smoothing and a debug panel are being tested.
